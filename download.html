Good to know

🌐Language

mind3D speaks English and German. On first start it asks which one you want; you can switch any time on the start screen under ℹ → Language.

💾Where are my mind maps?

Only on your device, in the app’s protected storage. On PC there is also a backup copy in your user folder. Use “Export” to back them up wherever you want.

🔄Updates

All mind maps are kept when updating. Before big changes, an export as a backup copy still helps.

🗑Uninstalling

Android: removing the app makes the system delete all of the app’s data. Windows: via “Apps” in Settings; your mind maps are kept. Exported files always stay where you put them.
