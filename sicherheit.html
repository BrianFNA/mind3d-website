Security

Your thoughts stay yours

Mind maps often hold the most personal things: plans, ideas, diaries, work. mind3D protects them in two stages. The basic protection is always on and costs no convenience. If you want more, switch on encryption.

Always on, for everyone

📴Fully offline

mind3D loads nothing from the internet and sends nothing there. The Android app does not even have the permission to.

🚫No trackers

No account, no ads, no analytics services. Checked: 0 of 432 known trackers.

☁️No cloud copy

App data ends up neither in the Google backup nor in the transfer to a new phone. Only what you export yourself is backed up.

📍Images without traces

GPS position, camera data and other hidden information is removed from images when inserting, importing and exporting.

🧪Checked imports

Every imported file is checked before opening. Manipulated mind maps cannot inject code.

🧱Sealed off

mind3D only shows its own pages, links open in the browser. The PC version runs on a current, hardened base (Electron 44).

🛡 Security Center

Encryption at the push of a button

On the start screen under 🛡. A wizard guides you through three steps: choose a password, write down the recovery words, enter three words as a check.

🔐 Encrypt everything

All mind maps and even their names are encrypted. On Android, mind3D unlocks automatically via your phone’s security chip, on PC with your password.

🗄 Vault

Only selected mind maps go into a tab of their own with a separate password. The vault locks after an adjustable time and as soon as you leave the app.

What else is included

📝Recovery words

24 words as a spare key in case you forget your password or switch devices. Write them down or store them in a password manager such as KeePass. The app spots typos immediately.

📦Encrypted export

Exports stay encrypted as .m3e. For sharing there is a separate export password. Modified or damaged files are detected.

👆App lock

mind3D only opens with your fingerprint, face or your phone’s screen lock (Android).

🖼Screen protection

No screenshots or screen recordings, hidden preview in the app overview.

👁Protection from snooping apps

From Android 14, other apps with accessibility rights cannot see your content. TalkBack keeps working.

⌨️Keyboard doesn’t learn along

Password and word fields have no suggestions and no autocorrect.

💽Encrypted backups

With encryption on, automatic backups are encrypted too and old unencrypted copies are deleted.

🔄Safe switch-over

If switching encryption on or off is interrupted, mind3D continues at the next start. Every mind map is checked afterwards.

⚠️Notice on rooted devices

On rooted or modified devices, mind3D points out once that protection is weaker there.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I forget my password?

You reset it with your 24 recovery words. Without the password and without the words, nobody can access the encrypted mind maps any more, not even the developers. So keep the words safe.

Do I have to switch on encryption?

No. The basic protection always applies. Encryption is worth it if others could get hold of your device or if your content is very personal.

Why don’t I need a password on Android?

With “Encrypt everything”, the key lives in your phone’s security chip and is only released on the unlocked device. For extra protection there is the app lock with fingerprint or the vault with its own password.

Can I take encrypted mind maps to a new device?

Yes. Export them as .m3e and open them on the new device. mind3D will ask for your recovery words there.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

By email to mind3D.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Security”. Reports are treated confidentially; details are only published after a fix.
